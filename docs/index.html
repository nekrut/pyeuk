Act I — The Scalability & Portability Barrier

When Classical MLST
Hits Eukaryotic Reality.

Traditional molecular typing frameworks were forged on haploid bacteria. When transferred to eukaryotic parasites characterized by sexual recombination, extensive within-host polyclonality, dynamic somy variation, and tandem-repeat arrays, they fail.

The Discrete Genotype Bottleneck
$$G_{ij} \in \{0, 1, 2\} \implies \text{Minor Clones Lost} \quad \& \quad \text{Uncatalogued Alleles } \to \text{Dropout}$$

Forcing continuous sequencing read depths into binary presence or discrete diploid genotypes discards multi-site physical phase, blinds surveillance to minor drug-resistant clones ($< 20\%$), and causes catastrophic typing dropouts whenever a specimen harbors novel uncatalogued alleles.

The Broken Status Quo

Catalogue-Bound MLST

  • × Requires pre-compiled static reference allele databases; uncatalogued mutations cause typing dropouts.
  • × Demands discrete diploid/haploid genotype calls, destroying intra-host minor clone dynamics.
  • × Discards physical phase across adjacent polymorphic sites within the same read.
  • × Bound to fragile custom laboratory pipelines; breaks on primer panel revisions.
Outcome: Inability to resolve multi-state outbreaks or track co-infecting parasite lineages.
The PyEuk Paradigm

Catalogue-Free Microhaplotyping

  • ✓ 100% data-adaptive window discovery directly from empirical CIGAR read alignments.
  • ✓ Continuous abundance-weighted allele sharing (wIBS) retaining minor clones down to 5% frequency.
  • ✓ Preserves physical phase across multi-codon intervals, capturing linked epistatic resistance.
  • ✓ Panel-independent architecture running across amplicon panels, target capture, and long-read data.
Breakthrough: Near-perfect outbreak separation (ARI = 0.9737) and 283 transmission cores across 8,325 surveillance genomes.
Act II — Algorithmic Foundation

The Four Mathematical Pillars
of Continuous Microhaplotyping.

From raw FASTQ reads to transmission cores: an end-to-end mathematical pipeline designed for complex eukaryote genomics.

1

Dynamic Window Placement

Sub-amplicon coordinate intervals are scored by maximizing haplotype width while enforcing balanced spanning read support:

Optimization Objective
$$S = \text{width} \times f_{\text{span}}, \quad f_{\text{span}} \ge 0.30$$

Strand-aware anchor shifts eliminate primer-initiation asymmetry, shifting anchors to recover dense windows with up to 97.6% spanning coverage.

2

Phase-Preserved Haplotypes

Only reads spanning the coordinate interval end-to-end contribute haplotypes. Physical phase across adjacent SNVs and indels is strictly preserved:

Quality & Abundance Thresholds
$$\text{MAPQ} \ge 20, \quad N_{\text{span}} \ge 30, \quad f_{\text{hap}} \ge 0.05$$

Indels are left-aligned and normalized. Low-abundance PCR noise is pruned while authentic biological minor clones are retained.

3

Continuous wIBS Metric

Genetic distance between specimens is computed using an allele-sharing distance weighted by intra-host abundance and locus ploidy:

Abundance-Weighted Distance
$$D(u, v) = 1 - \frac{\sum_{w \in W_{uv}} \frac{1}{m_w} \sum_{h \in H_w} \min(a_{uwh}, a_{vwh})}{|W_{uv}|}$$

Locus ploidy $m_w$ normalizes nuclear ($m_w=2$) and mitochondrial ($m_w=1$) markers, avoiding forced diploid calling.

4

Bootstrapped Stability Sweep

Rather than picking an arbitrary tree cut height, PyEuk evaluates co-clustering probability across a continuous distance sweep:

Monte Carlo Stability Criteria
$$B = 200, \quad \text{Subsample} = 0.85, \quad p_{ij} \ge 0.90$$

Connected-component transmission cores are extracted at high consensus support ($|2p - 1| \ge 0.50$), separating stable transmission chains from background noise.

Act III — Computational Arithmetic & Scaling

Vectorized Array Kernels:
From Days to Minutes.

Complex likelihood-based Markov solvers or MCMC samplers take days to cluster thousands of eukaryotic genomes. PyEuk computes complete 8,325-specimen distance matrices in 4 minutes.

Zero External Dependencies

Numba-Accelerated C-Speed Kernels

Microhaplotype matrices are encoded as sparse presence/absence tensors. Pairwise distance calculations are dispatched to JIT-compiled Numba C kernels with automatic multi-threading.

No external relational database, no BLAST or HMMER dependencies, and no proprietary software wrappers. Pure, reproducible Python.

Act V — Biological Reality

Empirical Proof Across
Diverse Parasite Systems.

Validated against gold-standard epidemiological tracebacks, national surveillance repositories, and international malaria clinical trials.

Foodborne Enteric Protozoan • 8,325 Genomes

Near-Perfect Outbreak Concordance

On the 2018 commercial produce outbreak (153 clinical cases investigated by FDA and CDC), PyEuk achieved near-perfect separation between commercial distributor clusters with Adjusted Rand Index ARI = 0.9737.

Across 8,325 nationwide surveillance isolates spanning seven consecutive years (2018–2024), PyEuk recovered 283 robust transmission cores (262 state-annotated), capturing persistent multi-state foodborne distribution chains while resolving cryptic species clades A, B, and C with ARI = 0.9848.

Apicomplexan Malaria • 91 Recurrence Pairs

Continental Origin & Recurrence Separation

In Plasmodium vivax (PvAmpSeq assay), PyEuk demonstrated 100% error-free continental separation between South American (Peru) and Western Pacific (Solomon Islands) isolates (ROC AUC = 1.000, ARI = 0.9712; 19 Peru cores, 32 Solomon Islands cores).

Across 91 patient recurrence pairs from a clinical trial, PyEuk cleanly discriminated homologous from heterologous recurrences (ROC AUC = 0.9080). On the 495-amplicon CDC AmpliSeq panel, unsupervised typing across 1,960 windows achieved ARI = 0.8063 concordance with curated transmission groups.

Veterinary Helminths & Emerging Fungi

Preserving Physical Phase for Drug Resistance

Unlike single-variant SNP pipelines that collapse adjacent polymorphic sites into unphased frequencies, PyEuk requires sequencing reads to span entire coordinate windows end-to-end.

This preserves physical phase across adjacent codons in gastrointestinal nematode targets (such as isotype-1 $\beta$-tubulin codons 167, 198, and 200) and emerging fungal pathogens (Candida auris ERG11 and FKS1), clarifying whether resistance mutations reside on the same or distinct circulating haplotypes.

Act VI — Rigorous Validation

Engineered for
Empirical Generalization.

Parameter choices are validated through repeated cross-validation and independent blind benchmarking, avoiding in-sample selection bias.

40-Fold Monte Carlo Cross-Validation
$$\text{Fixed Default }(N_{\text{span}} \ge 30): \quad \text{Held-Out Mean ARI} = 0.8608 \quad (\text{Median} = 0.9237)$$

Evaluated across 40 independent random Monte Carlo splits (65% train / 35% test; $n_{\text{test}} = 53$). The default threshold fixed in advance achieves performance statistically indistinguishable from split-specific tuning (mean ARI 0.8950), demonstrating exceptional parameter stability on unseen test cohorts.
